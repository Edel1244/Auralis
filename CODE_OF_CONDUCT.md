# Code of Conduct

AURALIS is a community built around music and code. We want it to be welcoming to everyone, regardless of age, body, ability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, experience level, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and assume good intent. Disagree about ideas, not people.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Credit others' work, and respect artists' rights, including in the music you share or test with.
- Help newcomers; everyone was new once.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and learn from them.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, intimidation, threats or stalking, online or offline.
- Discriminatory jokes, slurs, or sexualized language and imagery.
- Personal attacks, trolling, or sustained disruption of discussions.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Sharing pirated content or encouraging copyright infringement.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, chat) and when someone represents the project in public. Operators of individual AURALIS instances set and enforce their own community rules for their users.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact the maintainers privately through the contact listed in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) (mark the subject "Conduct"). Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers who are involved in a report step aside from handling it.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may take any action they find appropriate, proportional to the behavior: a private warning, a public warning, temporary restrictions, or a permanent ban from project spaces. We aim to explain decisions to the people affected.

Thank you for helping keep AURALIS a good place to make and share music.
